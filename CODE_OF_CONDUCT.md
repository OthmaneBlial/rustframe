# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in RustFrame welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent while discussing ideas, give actionable technical feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and keep criticism focused on the work. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants when necessary. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is officially representing RustFrame elsewhere. It is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
